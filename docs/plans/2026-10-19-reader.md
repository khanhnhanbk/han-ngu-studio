# Reading assistant: "Đọc hiểu" / "Reader" (`/tools/reader`)

## Context

The owner asked for "something awesome", picked a reading assistant, and asked to study the top tools first. Learners paste any Chinese text and read it with help:
- words coloured by HSK level;
- tap a word for a card with Pinyin, Hán Việt, both meanings, the HSK level and links to other tools;
- a difficulty estimate for the text;
- known-word tracking;
- one-click hand-off of new words to Anki, Quiz and the practice sheet.

### What the top tools do (research, Oct 2026)

| Tool | What we adopt |
|---|---|
| **Du Chinese / HSKStory** (graded readers) | Tap a word for Pinyin, definition and HSK. **Smart Pinyin**: show Pinyin only for words above the reader's level (modes: smart / always / hidden). Underline HSK words. Simplified/Traditional switch. |
| **studycli Chinese Text Analyzer** | **Estimated HSK level = the lowest level whose words cover ≥ 95 % of the text.** Per-level distribution chart. Colour-coded annotated text. Word table (word, Pinyin, HSK, frequency, meaning). |
| **Chinese Text Analyser (imron), LingQ** | **Known-word tracking**: mark words known, see "% of this text you know", and export unknown words **sorted by frequency** for flashcards. LingQ's blue = new / known states. |
| **Zhongwen popup, Pleco reader, Readibu** | Paste or import your own text (.txt), pop-up lookup, a word list exported for Anki. |
| **Cathoven / Smart Chinese Reader** | Word-by-word HSK tags and per-level counts. |

**Where we go further.** Things none of them combine:
- **Hán Việt** for every word, with the reading chosen in context;
- **Vietnamese meanings** from our HSK 3.0 list;
- **HSK 3.0 with 9 levels** (many analyzers still use HSK 2.0, levels 1–6);
- 100 % in the browser (no text sent; most competitors are server or app based);
- a direct hand-off to our Anki, Quiz and practice-sheet tools.

**Left out on purpose:**
- **Sentence translation** (Du Chinese) would need a translation service at runtime, which CLAUDE.md forbids.
- **Read-aloud**: later, local voices only (owner decision).
- **Grammar-point detection**: no data source.

### Owner's decisions

- **Known words are remembered on this device**, in localStorage, never sent anywhere, with TSV export/import and a clear button. This is a new hard-rule-4 exception, like the Chiết tự memory stories. Record it in CLAUDE.md.
- v1 extras: **Smart Pinyin**, **word table with frequency**, **estimated HSK level (95 % rule)**. No read-aloud.

## 1. Pure logic: `lib/reader/` (tested)

- **`analyze.ts`**: `analyzeText(data, text)` returns lines of tokens:
  - `{ kind: "word", text, simp, pinyin, hanviet, level: HskLevel | null, meaningVi, meaningEn }`
  - `{ kind: "other", text }`
  - It reuses `analyzeLine` (`lib/hanviet/sentence.ts`: segmentation, whole-line Pinyin, Hán Việt), `vocabFor` (`lib/vocab/lookup.ts`: level + Vietnamese meaning, matched by Pinyin) and `meanings[word]` (English, `lib/anki/load.ts`).
  - Tokens without Han characters are `other`.
- **`stats.ts`**:
  - `levelStats(lines)`: word-token and distinct-word counts per level (1 … 6, 7-9, outside HSK).
  - `estimateLevel(stats)`: the lowest level L whose cumulative token share is ≥ 0.95; otherwise "7-9+" (harder than HSK 9).
  - `knownShare(lines, isKnown)`: the share of word tokens the reader knows.
  - `wordTable(lines)`: distinct words in first-appearance order, with count and data. Sorting is done in the UI.
  - `isKnown(word)` = in the known list **or** (option on) the word's level ≤ "Trình độ của tôi".
- **`known.ts`** (pattern of `components/decompose/NotesBox.tsx` storage):
  - known-word list keyed by the Simplified form, so 學習 and 学习 count as one word;
  - localStorage key `reader-known`, all reads and writes in try/catch;
  - TSV export/import (`word<TAB>date`);
  - a `useKnownWords()` hook with add, remove, clear and import.
- **`handoff.ts`**: `wordsHash(words)` / `parseWordsHash(hash)` read and write `#words=…`, in the hash, never the query string.

## 2. Page: `app/tools/reader/page.tsx` (client)

**Input**
- textarea (5000 code points maximum), `.txt` upload, and the **Đọc** button;
- **Trình độ của tôi** (HSK 1 … 7-9, default 3) and a toggle **Coi mọi từ đến cấp này là đã biết**; both saved in localStorage as preferences.

**Reading view**
- Each word is a focusable button with a coloured HSK underline.
- New words (not known) are highlighted in brand colour. Known words are plain.
- **Smart Pinyin** has three modes:
  - **Thông minh**: ruby only on words that are not known;
  - **Luôn hiện**;
  - **Ẩn**.
  It renders ruby the same way `RubyLine` does in `app/tools/pinyin/page.tsx`.

**Word card**
- Opened by tap or Enter: a popover on desktop, a bottom sheet on phones; Esc closes it.
- Contents: the word, Pinyin, Hán Việt, HSK badge, Vietnamese and English meanings, and how many times it appears in this text.
- Actions:
  - **Đã biết / Bỏ đã biết**
  - **+ Danh sách học**
  - **Thứ tự nét** (`#c=`)
  - **Chiết tự** (`#c=`)

**Summary**
- **Ước tính: HSK N** (95 % rule), with a one-line explanation.
- Per-level bars (CSS) with %.
- **Bạn biết X % số từ** (known share).
- Totals: words and distinct words.

**Word table**
- Columns: word, Pinyin, Hán Việt, HSK, meaning, count, known toggle.
- Filters: **Tất cả / Từ mới**. Sort by order of appearance, frequency or level.
- **Thêm các từ mới vào danh sách học**, adding the new words by frequency.

**Danh sách học** (session)
- **→ Tạo thẻ Anki** (`/tools/anki#words=`)
- **→ Trắc nghiệm** (`/tools/quiz#words=`)
- **→ Phiếu tập viết** (`#chars=`)
- **Tải .tsv**
- **Xóa**

**Known words box**
- count, **Tải danh sách (TSV)**, **Nhập từ file TSV**, **Xóa tất cả** (with a confirm);
- a privacy note in the same style as the Chiết tự notes.

**Shared behaviour**
- **Script**: same as `app/tools/hanviet/page.tsx`. Analyse `conv.toSimp(text)` and relabel the shown words with `createAligner`. Lookups and the known list use the Simplified form.
- **Download gate**: `confirmDownload([...TOKENIZER_FILES, HANVIET_FILE, VOCAB_FILE, MEANINGS_FILE])`, about 1.3 MB.
- **Level colours**: one hue per level for the underline and dot, grey for "outside HSK". Text stays `ink-*`. Check light and dark mode and another accent.
- **Links**: all via `useHref()`.

## 3. Hand-off in Anki and Quiz

`app/tools/anki/page.tsx` and `app/tools/quiz/page.tsx` read `#words=` on mount (`parseWordsHash`) and fill their textarea. This mirrors `#chars=` in `app/tools/practice-sheet/page.tsx`.

## Tests

- **`lib/reader/reader.test.ts`** (real data and tokenizer):
  - `我们明天去银行取钱。`: segmentation, `yínháng` (not `yínxíng`), `ngân hàng`, levels from `vocab.json`;
  - punctuation is `other`; a non-HSK word gives `null`;
  - `estimateLevel`: boundary cases (exactly 95 %, all outside HSK);
  - `knownShare` with a list and with the level option;
  - `wordTable` counts and order;
  - known-list TSV round trip;
  - `wordsHash` round trip (Traditional characters, newlines, non-BMP);
  - known list keyed by the Simplified form (學習 → 学习).
- The i18n tests cover the new keys.
