CVDICT — Từ điển Hán Việt CVDICT (Chinese-Vietnamese dictionary)
https://github.com/ph0ngp/CVDICT, commit c379d909e308343a247e51619f7839a2060a271c
Author: Phong Phan. A Vietnamese translation of CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict), made mostly by a
fine-tuned ChatGPT-4o model and checked by hand; it may still contain errors.

Used in Hán Ngữ Studio (scripts/build-vocab-source.mjs, scripts/lib/cvdict.mjs):
- the Vietnamese meanings and measure words in assets/dictionary/vocab_hsk30.csv
  and public/data/vocab.json. Changes: senses limited to the first three per
  entry, classifier ("LT:") notes moved to their own column, joined with "; ".
- the whole dictionary in assets/dictionary/cvdict.tsv and public/data/cvdict/.
  Changes: "variant of X" senses resolved to X's senses, classifier notes moved
  to their own column, Pinyin written with tone marks, entries that differ only
  in their Traditional form merged.
These derived files are released under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
